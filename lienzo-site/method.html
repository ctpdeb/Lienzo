For clinicians

What we ask of an advisor

  1. 01

    Initial discovery · 15 minutes

    A brief call or email to name the two or three highest-yield concepts you wish you had a handout for in clinic.

  2. 02

    Asynchronous review

    Reviewing one or two visual drafts by email, with clinical edits executed directly in the artwork.

  3. 03

    Recognition

    Advisors and their institutions are credited on all distributed materials, digital portfolios, and public health exhibitions.

Built with, not for.

Every Lienzo resource exists because a clinician told us exactly where families get stuck. The advisory role stays frictionless: one short call, one or two rounds of async feedback, nothing more.
