The Lienzo Method

A repeatable product-development pipeline, so accuracy and effectiveness are provable, not just claimed.

método

Eight steps, every time

Each guide moves from a clinician's real pain point to a validated, distributed piece of artwork, and then back again with feedback.

  1. Identify

    Interview clinicians and families to surface real pain points.

  2. Map

    Pinpoint exactly where misunderstanding happens.

  3. Translate

    Convert the concept into visual language.

  4. Validate

    Clinician review for clinical accuracy.

  5. Pilot

    Test with the families it's made for.

  6. Measure

    Track comprehension, confidence, and recall.

  7. Deploy

    Distribute through hospitals and clinics.

  8. Iterate

    Real feedback shapes the next version.
